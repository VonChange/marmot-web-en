FAQ

A few useful answers.

Is Marmot a TV remote or an ADB tool?

Both. The phone app gives you a touch remote and fast typing, while ADB enables deeper TV tools such as app management and device utilities.

Which devices are supported?

Most Android TV devices, smart TVs, and TV boxes that allow ADB debugging can be used. The phone app supports iOS and Android.

Does it replace every TV remote?

It is designed for Android TV control through network and ADB features. Device compatibility depends on the TV system and enabled permissions.
