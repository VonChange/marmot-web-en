## Marmot TV Browser

2026.11.16 · 64-bit ARM (arm64-v8a)

A Gecko-based web browser for Android TV and TV boxes, designed to work with **Browser Remote in the Marmot TV Remote mobile app**. Browse on the big screen while using your phone for navigation, typing and playback controls.

- **Phone as a remote:** Move the pointer, tap links, drag to scroll and enter text from your phone.
- **Open websites on TV:** Send URLs and searches from your phone, and open saved bookmarks without typing with the TV remote.
- **Video controls:** Play or pause supported videos, seek, adjust volume and playback speed, and switch to full screen from your phone.
- **Browser tools:** Manage built-in ad blocking, userscripts and TV downloads from the mobile app.

To connect, open Marmot TV Browser on your TV, keep your phone and TV on the same local network, then open **Browser Remote** in Marmot TV Remote and select your TV.
