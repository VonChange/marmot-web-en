Using the app

Marmot TV Remote lets you control compatible TVs and TV boxes and cast local media to supported devices. Universal Remote uses Wi-Fi without ADB. The separate ADB features, including Air Mouse, screen control, and TV app and file management, require a compatible Android-based TV or TV box and authorization on that device.

Use the app only with devices, networks, files, and media that you own or have permission to access or control. Review any requested pairing, debugging, installation, or file operation before approving it. Third-party apps and content may have their own terms.

Google Play purchases and subscriptions

The app offers an optional auto-renewing annual subscription and a separate one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play shows the price and purchase terms before you confirm and processes the payment through your Google account.

  • Annual subscription. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it before the next renewal. Manage or cancel it in Google Play Subscriptions. Canceling normally lets you keep access until the paid period ends. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.
  • One-time lifetime purchase. This option unlocks the features described at checkout for a single payment and does not renew.
  • Restore purchases. Use the Google account that made the purchase and select Restore Purchases in the app. Contact us if a purchase does not appear.
