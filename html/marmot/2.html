Privacy Policy for Marmot PC Service Browser Extension

生效日期：2026年7月12日 运营方：个人（冯昌义）
隐私摘要 本扩展仅与用户本机安装的土拨鼠电脑服务通信，不将浏览历史、媒体播放记录或账号数据上传到开发者服务器，不出售用户数据，也不用于广告画像。

中文版

一、适用范围与单一用途

本隐私政策适用于“土拨鼠电脑服务”浏览器扩展（以下简称“本扩展”）。本扩展是土拨鼠电脑服务的配套组件，用于接收用户本机土拨鼠电脑服务发送的控制指令，在用户主动打开的媒体网页中执行播放、暂停、进度、音量、全屏、弹幕及站点适配操作，并将必要的播放器状态返回给本机服务。

本扩展不提供、存储或转播第三方媒体内容，也不代表任何电视台、视频网站或内容平台。

二、本扩展处理的信息

为实现媒体遥控功能，本扩展可能在浏览器本地处理以下信息：

  • 当前标签页的 URL、标签页标识和活动状态，用于定位需要控制的媒体页面并避免重复打开标签页。
  • 页面中的媒体播放器状态，例如是否播放、当前进度、总时长、音量、静音、倍速、清晰度和全屏状态。
  • 页面中与媒体控制相关的 DOM 元素及功能状态，例如播放器按钮、弹幕开关和站点公开显示的登录状态。
  • 控制命令的执行结果、连接状态和错误信息，用于向本机服务反馈操作是否成功。

本扩展不会读取用户输入的账号密码、支付信息、表单内容、Cookie 内容或与媒体控制无关的私人通信。

三、权限使用说明

  • nativeMessaging：用于与用户主动安装的土拨鼠电脑服务本机程序通信。通信通过浏览器提供的 Native Messaging 接口完成。
  • tabs：用于查找当前媒体标签页、读取必要的页面地址以及复用或更新现有媒体标签页。
  • scripting：用于将扩展包内随附的媒体适配脚本注入受支持页面。扩展不会下载并执行远程 JavaScript。
  • 网站访问权限：用于识别媒体播放器并执行用户发出的控制指令。部分播放器位于 iframe 中，因此扩展可能需要在页面子框架中运行。

四、数据传输、存储与日志

扩展与土拨鼠电脑服务之间的控制消息通过用户本机 Native Host 传输。Native Host 连接本机回环地址上的 pc-remote 服务，该通信不会发送到开发者云端服务器。

为解析用户主动打开的媒体地址，本扩展可能直接请求对应第三方媒体网站的页面或媒体资源；这些请求受第三方网站自身隐私政策约束。

扩展本身不建立云端用户档案。土拨鼠电脑服务可能在用户电脑本地日志中记录运行事件、页面 URL、执行结果和错误上下文，以便排查问题。日志不会由本扩展自动上传，用户可以通过土拨鼠电脑服务控制台打开并删除本地日志。

五、数据共享与出售

我们不出售、出租或交换用户数据，不将用户数据用于定向广告或跨站跟踪，也不向第三方数据经纪商提供浏览历史。除用户主动访问的第三方媒体网站外，本扩展不会把页面信息发送给其他第三方服务。

六、第三方网站

本扩展可控制用户主动访问的第三方媒体页面。第三方网站可能独立收集信息，并适用其自身隐私政策和服务条款。我们不控制第三方网站的数据处理行为，也不对第三方内容的可用性、合法性或准确性作出保证。

七、数据安全

本扩展仅允许清单中授权的扩展身份访问 Native Host，并使用浏览器提供的权限和隔离机制运行。所有业务脚本随扩展包发布，不通过远程下载更换可执行代码。我们会在合理范围内更新扩展和本机服务，以修复已知安全问题。

八、用户选择、撤销权限和删除数据

  • 用户可以随时在 Microsoft Edge 或其他 Chromium 浏览器的扩展管理页禁用或卸载本扩展。
  • 用户可以停止或卸载土拨鼠电脑服务，从而终止本扩展与本机服务的通信。
  • 用户可以删除土拨鼠电脑服务在本机生成的日志和配置文件。
  • 卸载扩展后，扩展将不再访问网页或接收本机控制指令。

九、未成年人保护

本扩展不以未成年人为特定目标，也不会主动收集年龄信息。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用本扩展及相关第三方媒体服务。

十、政策更新

当扩展功能、权限或数据处理方式发生重要变化时，我们会更新本政策及生效日期。涉及新增敏感权限或重要数据用途时，将通过商店说明、扩展更新提示或产品页面向用户说明。

English Version

1. Scope and Single Purpose

This Privacy Policy applies to the Marmot PC Service browser extension (the “Extension”). The Extension is a companion component of Marmot PC Service. It receives commands from the software installed on the user's computer, performs media controls such as play, pause, seek, volume, fullscreen, danmaku, and site-specific actions on media pages intentionally opened by the user, and returns necessary playback status to the local service.

The Extension does not provide, store, or retransmit third-party media content and is not affiliated with any television station, video website, or content platform.

2. Information Processed by the Extension

To provide media remote-control functions, the Extension may process the following information locally in the browser:

  • The current tab URL, tab identifier, and active state, to locate the media page and avoid opening duplicate playback tabs.
  • Media player status, including playback state, current time, duration, volume, mute state, playback speed, quality, and fullscreen state.
  • DOM elements and feature states needed for media control, such as player buttons, danmaku controls, and login status visibly exposed by a supported website.
  • Command results, connection state, and error information required to report whether an operation succeeded.

The Extension does not read account passwords, payment information, form contents, cookie contents, or private communications unrelated to media control.

3. Permission Usage

  • nativeMessaging: Used to communicate with the Marmot PC Service native application intentionally installed by the user.
  • tabs: Used to locate the current media tab, read the necessary page URL, and reuse or update an existing media tab.
  • scripting: Used to inject media adapters bundled with the Extension into supported pages. The Extension does not download and execute remote JavaScript.
  • Host access: Used to detect media players and perform commands requested by the user. Some players are embedded in iframes, so the Extension may run in page subframes.

4. Data Transmission, Storage, and Logs

Control messages between the Extension and Marmot PC Service are transmitted through a Native Host on the user's computer. The Native Host connects to the pc-remote service on a local loopback address. These messages are not sent to the developer's cloud servers.

When resolving a media address intentionally opened by the user, the Extension may directly request pages or media resources from the relevant third-party media website. Such requests are governed by the third party's own privacy policy.

The Extension does not create a cloud user profile. Marmot PC Service may record operational events, page URLs, command results, and error context in local log files for troubleshooting. The Extension does not automatically upload these logs. Users can open and delete local logs through the Marmot PC Service console.

5. Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. We do not use user data for targeted advertising or cross-site tracking, and we do not provide browsing history to data brokers. Except for third-party media websites intentionally visited by the user, the Extension does not send page information to other third-party services.

6. Third-Party Websites

The Extension can control third-party media pages intentionally visited by the user. Third-party websites may independently collect information under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their data practices and do not guarantee the availability, legality, or accuracy of third-party content.

7. Data Security

The Native Host only accepts extension identities listed in its allowed origins. The Extension runs using browser permission and isolation mechanisms. All business logic is included in the submitted package and is not replaced by remotely downloaded executable code. We update the Extension and native service as reasonably necessary to address known security issues.

8. User Choices and Data Deletion

  • Users may disable or uninstall the Extension at any time from the extension management page in Microsoft Edge or another Chromium browser.
  • Users may stop or uninstall Marmot PC Service to terminate communication with the Extension.
  • Users may delete logs and configuration files generated locally by Marmot PC Service.
  • After uninstalling the Extension, it no longer accesses webpages or receives local control commands.

9. Children

The Extension is not specifically directed to children and does not actively collect age information. Children should use the Extension and related third-party media services under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this policy when the Extension's functions, permissions, or data practices materially change. We will update the effective date and provide appropriate notice through the store listing, extension update information, or product website.
