1. App Overview

Marmot ADB is a local-network ADB utility for compatible TVs and TV boxes. It provides device discovery and pairing, shell commands, app and file management, and supported remote and screen-control functions.

Your phone or tablet is the controller. App installation, shell commands and TV file access apply to the connected external device. You must authorize the connection on that device and should only connect to devices you own or have permission to manage.

No app account or login is required for ADB tools.

Connection records, preferences and error logs are stored locally.

ADB file transfers and controls use the authorized device connection.

Crash and error reports are not automatically uploaded to our server.

2. Data We Process

2.1 Device connections and local settings

The app processes device names, IP addresses and ports, device identifiers, system information, connection history and pairing credentials to discover, connect to and remember your authorized devices. Preferences, display settings and related caches are stored on your phone or tablet.

The app also reads or creates an app device identifier and reads basic device model and system-version information. The identifier is not an account name or email address. On iOS, the identifier is stored in the system Keychain; it may also appear in local diagnostic records or information you choose to copy or share.

2.2 Files, apps and screen content

When you choose to browse, upload, download, preview or install a file, the app processes the selected file, its name, path, size and related metadata. Images, recordings, previews and temporary copies may be stored locally to complete the operation.

App management processes information about apps on the connected external device. Where supported on Android, choosing to install an app from your phone also reads local installed-app information and the selected APK so it can be transferred to the external device.

Screen-control features process the connected device's screen, supported audio, input events and commands. These operations use the authorized ADB connection; the app does not automatically upload TV files, screen content or shell commands to our server.

Files and photos can contain personal information, including embedded location metadata. Review the content and destination before transferring or sharing them.

2.3 Local diagnostics

Crash and error information is saved locally for troubleshooting. Logs may contain error messages, stack traces, device and connection details, identifiers, file paths or parameters relevant to an operation. You can view, clear, copy or share available logs through the app. Review them before sending them to another person.

2.4 Online requests and support

The iOS app retrieves version-update and configuration information from our static configuration service. Opening help, support, privacy-policy or App Store links also makes network requests. The receiving service necessarily receives connection information such as your IP address and requested URL, and may process standard operational or security logs.

If you contact us or voluntarily send diagnostic material, we receive the contact details, message and attachments you provide. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue. There is no email-binding or membership-restoration feature in Marmot ADB.

3. Permissions

Permissions and availability vary by operating system and device. The app uses access relevant to the feature you choose:

3.1 Network and local network

Used to discover compatible devices, pair and connect over ADB, transfer files and control the connected device. Internet access is also used for the online requests described above. A local ADB connection normally requires your phone or tablet and the target device to be on the same local network.

3.2 Wi-Fi state and nearby devices

Used for network information and local-device discovery where supported. Some Android versions require nearby Wi-Fi or location permissions for these network-discovery operations.

3.3 Photos, files and storage

Used when you select local files or images for transfer, preview or save downloaded content, or save images to the system photo library. Access is subject to the file or photo choices and permissions provided by your operating system.

3.4 Camera and location

The photo-selection components include camera-capture and photo-location-metadata capabilities. If you use a photo-capture flow, camera access is used to take a photo for the selected operation, and while-in-use location access may be used to include related location metadata in that photo. The app does not require background or always-on location access.

3.5 Android app visibility, installation and USB access

Where the corresponding tools are available, Android app visibility is used to list local apps for the install-from-phone feature, and installation or file access supports the package operations you choose. USB device access is used only when you select a supported USB/OTG connection. These platform-specific capabilities are not available on every device or operating system.

You can manage app permissions in your system settings. Refusing an optional permission may make the related feature unavailable. Pairing and ADB authorization on the external device are managed separately in that device's settings.

4. Sharing and Online Services

Files, commands and control data are sent to the external device you connect to for the operation you request. The app does not provide an account-based cloud file-storage or subscription-verification service.

Operating-system file and photo pickers, sharing services and the App Store process information according to the relevant platform's policies. Websites or services you open through a link receive their own network requests and apply their own privacy policies.

If you choose to send information to us for support, it is used to handle your request. Do not include passwords, private files or other sensitive information unless needed for the issue you want us to investigate.

Use ADB only with authorized devices and trusted networks. Access to files and control operations depends on the external device's permissions and the selected connection method.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local records and caches are retained to support device reconnection, your preferences and the operations you perform. Available controls include:

  • Using the app's cache-cleanup settings and selecting the categories you want to remove, such as temporary files, diagnostic logs or device history.
  • Deleting exported files, downloaded content, recordings or saved photos from their storage location or the system photo library.
  • Removing the app to delete data held in its local app container, subject to operating-system backup and storage behavior.
  • Managing or revoking ADB pairing and authorization on the external TV or TV box.

Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove iOS Keychain entries, system backups, files saved outside the app or photos in the system library. It also does not remove apps or files already transferred to a TV, or revoke authorization stored by that TV.

If you have sent us support correspondence or diagnostic material and want it deleted, contact us using the email below. We use support material for handling the request and related follow-up, and will address deletion requests subject to any applicable legal retention obligations.

6. Children and Policy Updates

Marmot ADB is not directed to children. If you are under the age required by your region to use digital services independently, use the app under the guidance of a parent or legal guardian. Managing external devices should be done with the device owner's authorization.

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
